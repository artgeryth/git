Yaya · Desktop Pet — Quick start
===============================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop or D:\ is fine).
   Please avoid C:\Program Files — she saves her memory next to the exe and that folder is read-only.

2. Double-click  娅娅桌面宠物.exe  (her main program).

3. She appears in the bottom-right corner of your desktop and stays on top of your windows.

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
  single click      pat her head (hop + hearts + affection +1)
  double click      open the menu   (right click works too)
  click her 4+ fast she pretends to get annoyed
  drag her          put her anywhere; she remembers the spot
  move your cursor near her   she notices you
  leave her alone   3 min: she tries to get your attention
                     5 min: she wanders off, bored
                     8 min: dozing off
                    12 min: fast asleep (and she wakes the moment you touch the keyboard)
  tray icon         let her rest / call her back / settings / quit

CHATTING
--------
  Menu → "Chat with me" (or double-click → Chat with me).
  Local library: fully offline, she answers from ~540 hand-written lines. Free, no account.
  AI chat:       Settings → Chat mode → AI chat, paste an API key (DeepSeek by default; any
                 OpenAI-compatible endpoint works) and press "Test connection".
                 Without a key she just keeps using the local library.

  In AI mode, interactions improvise too: patting her, feeding her cake and watching her do a
  trick get a freshly written line instead of the same fixed one. The local line still plays
  first, so it never feels laggy. Can be switched off in Settings.

  Switch language anytime: Settings → Language → 中文 / English.

THINGS YOU CAN TYPE AT HER
--------------------------
  rock paper scissors   play a round — or pick it from her menu (right-click her)
                        she keeps score, gloats, makes excuses, and cheats ~8% of the time
  guess a number        she picks a number from 1 to 100 and you guess it
  stop                  end the game you're in
  remember: <fact>      she keeps it        (e.g.  remember: I'm studying for exams)
  what do you remember  she reads the list back
  forget everything     wipe it
  my birthday is 3/15   she'll remember the date and say something special that day

SHE LOOKS AFTER YOU
-------------------
  50 minutes straight at the computer  →  she tells you to get up and move.
  Still awake between 1am and 5am      →  she tells you to go to bed.
  Timing uses only Windows' "last input time" — nothing you type is recorded.
  Turn it off in Settings ("Break & late-night reminders").

  She also knows what day it is: New Year, New Year's Eve, Valentine's, Children's Day,
  National Day, Halloween, Christmas, Spring Festival and Mid-Autumn — plus your birthday.
  (The lunar dates table runs to 2030.)

HER FILES
---------
  data\state.json   her memory: name, affection, position, size, settings,
                    the things you asked her to remember, and your birthday
                    (an API key, if you added one, is encrypted with Windows DPAPI)
  data\pet.log      diagnostics log, useful if something looks wrong

  She never records what you type and never reads your files. Without AI mode she is fully offline.

UNINSTALL
---------
  1. Tray icon → Quit
  2. Delete this folder and the desktop shortcut
  3. If you ticked "Start with Windows", also delete this registry value:
     HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run  ->  YayaDesktopPet

WINDOWS MIGHT WARN YOU
----------------------
  The exe is not code-signed. If you see "Windows protected your PC":
  click "More info" → "Run anyway".
  To avoid the prompt entirely: right-click the downloaded zip → Properties → tick "Unblock" → OK,
  then extract.

USING YOUR OWN CHARACTER
------------------------
  content\sprites\ holds her look. Put your own 3-view sheet in content\sheet\ and run:
      tools\make-sprites.exe content\sheet\your-sheet.jpg content\sprites "59-376,392-791,809-1226"
  (omit the last argument to auto-detect). If content\sprites\ is missing, she falls back to the
  original placeholder mascot in content\placeholder\.
